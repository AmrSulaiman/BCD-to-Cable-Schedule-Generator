BCD to Cable Schedule

Drop the block cable diagrams for one schedule. The tool reads every cable reference, traces each cable to its source and destination, merges cables shown on more than one sheet, and fills the company cable schedule template.

Runs on this computer. Drawings are read inside your browser. They are only sent out if you run the optional AI cross-check.

    Voltage comes from the band: B1 = 24V, B2 and B1&B2 = 400V. Length, CPC type, installation method and comments are left blank.

    When it runs, close-up images of parts of the drawings are sent to Google's Gemini service. On Google's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products. The rules engine's reading never leaves this computer.

    Only the unsure readings plus a few spot checks are sent, so it usually takes well under a minute. If a spot check disagrees, the rest of that sheet is checked too. The schedule is ready to use straight away; the AI results are added when they arrive.
